4 laws Bangladesh needs.

Three proposed statutes and an AI strategy. These are our recommendations, not enacted law.

Our proposal

Online Safety Act

Unified protection from online harms

Enact a single Online Safety Act — a unified, systems/risk-based framework covering both harmful content and cybercrime; lets the CPA, Pornography Act and BTA-online reach be repealed/narrowed

platform duties of carenotice-and-actiontransparency reportingsafety-by-designprotection for vulnerable groupsproportionate safe-harbour

Our proposal

Regulation of Investigatory Powers Act

Judicial authorization and oversight

Enact a Regulation of Investigatory Powers Act — consolidate surveillance powers now scattered across CrPC 1898, Special Powers Act 1974, BTA, Anti-Terrorism Act, and non-public agency mandates into one law with real safeguards

prior judicial authorization with narrow emergency exceptions and prompt reviewprohibit bulk surveillance except under exceptional statutory safeguardsindependent oversighteffective remediestransparency reportingcontrols on surveillance technology procurement

Digital Commerce Act

Rules for contemporary digital commerce

Enact a Digital Commerce Act — a technology-neutral e-commerce law covering B2C/B2B/C2C/C2B/B2B2C, super-apps and social commerce
