Your everyday life

How it affects you.

Start with the situation, then distinguish the legal rule from our assessment of its consequences.

You keep consensual adult intimate material privately.

The law

Sections 4 and 8(7) broadly prohibit production, storage, possession and distribution of pornography. The framework does not exclude consensual adult material kept for private, non-commercial use. Section 6 provides search and seizure powers.

Our analysis · What this means for you

The consequences extend beyond public distribution: private material and devices can come within enforcement. Vague definitions and narrow exemptions also create uncertainty for sexual-health education, artistic work and other legitimate expression.

An allegation involves intimate-image abuse or child sexual abuse material.

The law

The statute overlaps with section 25 of the Cyber Protection Act, but carries a different penalty framework. Its offences are cognizable and non-bailable: police can arrest without a warrant, and bail depends on judicial discretion.

Our analysis · What this means for you

The legal route can change the penalties and arrest or bail rules attached to overlapping conduct. “Non-bailable” does not mean bail is impossible. The failure to separate consensual content from exploitation also weakens a focused response to serious sexual harms.

Offenses

Our analysis

Where the framework falls short.

A vague, overbroad ban that fails to separate consensual adult content from real harms (CSAM, TFSV, non-consensual/deepfake); overlaps the Cyber Protection Act

Definitions

The definition of 'pornography' (s.2(c)) turns on vague terms — 'sexually suggestive', 'obscene', 'semi-nude', 'sexually arousing'

Replace or amend.

Our recommendationOur proposed wording (excerpt)

"pornography" means any material or content, in any medium, that expressly and predominantly depicts or describes, of or related to a person, any real or simulated sexually explicit acts ... which lacks significant literary, research, artistic, political, cultural, historical, religious, educational, media reporting, law enforcement or criminal investigation, medical, or scientific value or purpose ... but excludes child sexual abuse material, non-consensual pornography, or digitally created pornography
