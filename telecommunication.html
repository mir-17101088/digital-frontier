Your everyday life

How it affects you.

Start with the situation, then distinguish the legal rule from our assessment of its consequences.

Your communications fall within an interception operation.

The law

Section 97A establishes a state-controlled interception platform under the Ministry of Home Affairs, accessible to multiple law-enforcement and intelligence agencies. Service providers must supply technical assistance. The review council examines interception after it occurs.

Our analysis · What this means for you

The powers reach communications content and associated data. Broad grounds and emergency exceptions can weaken oversight, putting the confidentiality of conversations, relationships and lawful activities at risk. After-the-fact review is different from independent permission before monitoring starts.

You run an internet-based service.

The law

The 2026 amendments extend the statute to online intermediaries and over-the-top services. Section 35 requires a licence for telecommunication services, but the expanded definitions leave uncertainty about how that requirement applies to digital platforms.

Our analysis · What this means for you

A platform could face licensing burdens and severe penalties designed for network operators. This is an uncertainty in the framework, not a settled claim that every website or app must obtain a telecom licence.

Scope & Licensing

New definitions pull social media, OTT and internet platforms under telecom regulation (ss.2(11),2(15),2(26a),2(2a),3,30)

Speech-Related Offenses

Speech offences duplicate the Cyber Protection Act: s.66 false/fraudulent messages (up to 5yr + BDT 490,000,000); s.66A broad 'national unity/economic interests' conduct (up to 5yr + BDT 990,000,000)

Surveillance & Interception

The enacted law downgraded prior authorization to after-the-fact review — surveillance can start first (s.97A)

Info Access & Disclosure

Sections 84 and 85 allow BTRC to require information and disclose information obtained during investigations. A hearing is allowed before confidential information is disclosed, but broad discretion over “public interest” and weak safeguards create risks for personal and business information.

Institutional Structure

BTRC is structurally subordinate to the executive; no explicit independence guarantee (ss.7,9,12,32,32A)
