Overlapping provisions can expose the same conduct to different penalty regimes.

Provisions overlap without identical offence definitions. “Not compared here” does not mean no legal coverage.

The overlap is a design problem.

Revenge porn and CSAM are punished under BOTH this Act and Cyber Protection Act s.25 — with conflicting penalties
