Scope and limits.

This edition does not comprehensively examine intellectual property, mobile and digital financial services, foreign exchange rules, digital-signature certification or critical-infrastructure protection. These topics may still appear where they intersect with the laws reviewed.

This edition reflects the research’s 2026 cutoff. It is not a live legislation tracker.

The classifications are indicative rather than exhaustive and should not be interpreted as definitive assessments of the current human rights impact of the legislation.
