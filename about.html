Research you can navigate.

A Tech Global Institute project, built to make Bangladesh’s digital-governance framework easier to understand.

TGI’s research, made navigable.

This is Tech Global Institute’s visual edition of A New Digital Frontier: A Roadmap for Rights-Respecting Digital Governance Laws in Bangladesh, second edition, 2026, by Shahzeb Mahmood.

Drawing on the detailed assessment in our white paper, this microsite offers an accessible, visual guide to eight key laws shaping digital governance in Bangladesh as of May 2026. It is not intended as a live legislative tracker or an exhaustive catalog of all applicable laws.

Scope and limits.

This edition does not comprehensively examine intellectual property, mobile and digital financial services, foreign exchange rules, digital-signature certification or critical-infrastructure protection. These topics may still appear where they intersect with the laws reviewed.

This edition reflects the research’s 2026 cutoff. It is not a live legislation tracker.

The classifications are indicative rather than exhaustive and should not be interpreted as definitive assessments of the current human rights impact of the legislation.
