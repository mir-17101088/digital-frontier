The security-first thread.

Read together, three laws give the state wide access to personal data, with limited independent oversight.

Three laws, one thread.

  1. 1

    Data Protection Act§24

    The state is exempted

    State processing can go ahead without consent on grounds including national security, public order and crime investigation.

  2. 2

    Data Management Act§§30(6), 32

    Records are joined

    National ID, passport, tax, address, birth-registration and driving-license databases connect to one national exchange.

  3. 3

    Telecommunication Act§97A

    Communications can be intercepted

    A central platform under the Ministry of Home Affairs is open to multiple agencies, and a review council looks only afterwards.

  4. =

    Read together

    What it adds up to

    A centralized surveillance architecture with broad scope, expansive state access and limited independent oversight.

  • 12The exemptions amplify concerns about the data authority’s independence.
  • 13The interception regime must be read together with the same exemptions.

What it means for you.

Exceptional access can become routine.

Exemptions meant to be exceptional may become normal and discretionary, risking arbitrary interference with privacy, expression and association.

Your records can add up to a profile.

Joined-up databases may enable highly granular, long-term citizen profiles, with risks of function creep and secondary use of personal data.

Monitoring can start before anyone independent approves it.

The review council no longer authorizes interception in advance, and an urgency exception allows interception without prior approval.

Follow the thread, law by law.

  1. 1

    Personal Data Protection Act 2026

    What protects you

    • Where an exemption applies, the other data-protection duties still do.§24(2)
    • Exemptions are restricted where they hit rights disproportionately or lack legal authority.§24(4)
    • The data authority may review misuse of an exemption.§24(5)

    Too wide

    Exemptions that open the door

    §24

    State authorities can invoke “national security,” “public interest” and “public order” to process personal data without the same compliance duties, which risks normalizing broad state access.

  2. 2

    National Data Management Act 2026

    What protects you

    • The Act declares the data authority independent.§27
    • Zero-trust architecture, privacy-preserving principles, auditability and minimization are named in the Act.§30

    Who decides

    An executive-centered data authority

    The Act calls the authority independent, but its governance is executive-centric: the policy board has 17 ministers, officials and appointees, and 7 government-nominated experts.

  3. 3

    Bangladesh Telecommunication Act 2001

    What protects you

    • Interception is subject to approval requirements and procedural safeguards against misuse.§97A
    • A review council examines interception after it takes place.§97A

    Order reversed

    Review that follows the interception

    §97A

    The enacted law downgraded prior authorization by an independent council to after-the-fact review, so surveillance can start first.

Who checks the power?

  1. Misuse of an exemption

    The data authority may review it.§24(5)

  2. The data authority

    Declared independent, yet the national data policy board has 17 ministers, parliamentarians, officials and appointees, and 7 government-nominated experts.§27

  3. Interception

    A review council examines it after it takes place.§97A

  4. Day-to-day monitoring

    The NTMC remains the principal monitoring body, within executive-controlled institutions.
